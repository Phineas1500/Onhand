A free ChatPDF alternative with no sign-up, for any webpage or PDF.

Onhand is a free, open-source AI Chrome extension that answers questions about whatever you're reading in Chrome, webpages and PDFs alike, without uploading files or pasting links. It highlights the passages it used right on the page.

Why read in place

  • No upload step: open a page or PDF and ask. Nothing to download first, and nothing to paste.
  • Pages behind a sign-in work: course sites, library journal access and docs you're signed in to, because Onhand reads the page in your browser.
  • The page becomes the answer: highlights and margin notes sit on the text you're reading, so you check each point where it appears.
  • More than one source: Onhand reads clearly related open tabs and highlights each claim in the tab it came from.
  • Built for learning: Learning mode asks guiding questions and quizzes you instead of only answering.

When ChatPDF may fit better

If most of your documents are Word or PowerPoint files, or you want to chat with them outside Chrome or on your phone, ChatPDF supports those. Onhand is a Chrome extension for webpages and PDFs.

Try it

Add Onhand to Chrome, open the page you're reading, and click the Onhand icon or press Ctrl+Shift+Space (⌘+Shift+Space on a Mac). Onhand Free needs no account or API key and has a daily limit; you can also sign in with your ChatGPT plan or use your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or OpenRouter key.
