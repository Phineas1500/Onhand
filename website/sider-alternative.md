# A Sider alternative built for reading and studying.

Onhand is a free, open-source AI Chrome extension for reading. Sider is an all-in-one AI sidebar for searching, reading and writing; Onhand focuses on the page in front of you. It highlights the passages behind each answer, leaves notes in the margin, and can tutor you with Learning mode.

[Add to Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ogjmncmkpgdkkcibdiacmagaehjohljb)

Free to start. No account needed. Open source. Also on [Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/onhand-ai/) and [Edge](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/icbhigkjjhkcheejmdfkfhiljnkommoj).

## How Onhand and Sider differ

|  | Onhand | Sider |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Focus | Reading and learning on the page you have open | An all-in-one AI sidebar for search, reading and writing, now with a browser agent |
| Answers | Highlights the passages it used on the page, with notes in the margin | Answers in the sidebar, or in a pop-up on text you select; its store listing doesn't mention highlighting the passages behind an answer |
| AI models | Onhand Free, your ChatGPT plan, or your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or OpenRouter key | Many models in one place, including GPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek and Grok, with group chat to compare them; bring your own OpenAI key |
| PDFs | Opens PDFs in its own viewer to highlight and annotate them | Chat with, summarize and translate PDFs |
| Learning | Learning mode: guiding questions, quizzes tied to the page, and a This session panel | Quick lookups that explain or translate words, and read-aloud |
| Price and code | Free tier with a daily limit, or your ChatGPT plan or own API key; open source | A free tier with daily credits; paid plans from $10 a month |

Sider details are from its [Chrome Web Store listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/sider-chat-with-all-ai-gp/difoiogjjojoaoomphldepapgpbgkhkb) and its [features](https://sider.ai/extensions), [pricing](https://sider.ai/pricing) and [help](https://sider.ai/help-center) pages, checked October 21, 2026. ## Built for the page you're reading

-   **Answers you can check:** every point is highlighted where it comes from, so you read the evidence instead of trusting a summary.
-   **Notes in the margin:** a sentence or two beside each highlight, so you keep your place on the page.
-   **Citations followed:** ask what a reference like \[14\] says, and Onhand opens the cited paper and answers from it.
-   **A tutor when you want one:** Learning mode asks before it tells and quizzes you on the page.

## When Sider fits better

Sider does much more outside reading: writing help in text boxes across sites, translating whole pages and PDFs, bilingual YouTube subtitles, AI answers alongside search engines, and comparing several models' answers side by side. It also has a browser agent that acts across sites, and it can change a page's layout or add summaries to it, saved per site. It runs in Edge and Safari too, with desktop and phone apps. If those are what you need most, Sider covers them; Onhand is focused on reading and learning.

## Try it

Add Onhand to Chrome, open the page you're reading, and click the Onhand icon or press `Ctrl+Shift+Space` (`⌘+Shift+Space` on a Mac). Onhand Free needs no account or API key and has a daily limit; you can also sign in with your ChatGPT plan or use your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or OpenRouter key.
