# A free ChatPDF alternative with no sign-up, for any webpage or PDF.

Onhand is a free, open-source AI Chrome extension that answers questions about whatever you're reading in Chrome, webpages and PDFs alike, without uploading files or pasting links. It highlights the passages it used right on the page.

[Add to Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ogjmncmkpgdkkcibdiacmagaehjohljb)

Free to start. No account needed. Open source. Also on [Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/onhand-ai/).

## How Onhand and ChatPDF differ

|  | Onhand | ChatPDF |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where you read | On the page itself in Chrome, with answers in a side panel | On ChatPDF's website or its desktop and phone apps, after you upload a file or paste a link |
| What it reads | Webpages and PDFs open in Chrome, including pages you're signed in to | PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Markdown and text files, and website or YouTube links you paste |
| Showing sources | Highlights the passages on the page, with notes in the margin | Citations that jump to the source in its side-by-side view |
| Price and code | Free tier with a daily limit, or your ChatGPT plan or own API key; open source | Free for 2 documents a day; ChatPDF Plus is unlimited ($14.99 a month on the US App Store) |

ChatPDF details are from its [website](https://www.chatpdf.com/) and its [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/chatpdf-chat-pdf-ai/id6742454061) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.chatpdf.android) listings, checked October 21, 2026. ChatPDF is a trademark of its owner; Onhand isn't affiliated with it.

## Why read in place

-   **No upload step:** open a page or PDF and ask. Nothing to download first, and nothing to paste.
-   **Pages behind a sign-in work:** course sites, library journal access and docs you're signed in to, because Onhand reads the page in your browser.
-   **The page becomes the answer:** highlights and margin notes sit on the text you're reading, so you check each point where it appears.
-   **More than one source:** Onhand reads clearly related open tabs and highlights each claim in the tab it came from.
-   **Built for learning:** Learning mode asks guiding questions and quizzes you instead of only answering.

## When ChatPDF may fit better

If most of your documents are Word or PowerPoint files, or you want to chat with them in a desktop or phone app, ChatPDF supports those. It also offers more tools around your files, such as an AI writer, flashcards and slides. Onhand is a browser extension for the webpages and PDFs you read.

## Try it

Add Onhand to Chrome, open the page you're reading, and click the Onhand icon or press `Ctrl+Shift+Space` (`⌘+Shift+Space` on a Mac). Onhand Free needs no account or API key and has a daily limit; you can also sign in with your ChatGPT plan or use your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or OpenRouter key.
