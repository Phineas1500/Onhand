# A SciSpace alternative for reading research papers in Chrome.

Onhand is a free, open-source AI Chrome extension for reading research papers and anything else you open in Chrome. Ask a question about the paper, and Onhand finds the passages that answer it, highlights them, and follows citations to the papers they cite.

[Add to Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ogjmncmkpgdkkcibdiacmagaehjohljb)

Free to start. No account needed. Open source. Also on [Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/onhand-ai/).

## How Onhand and SciSpace differ

|  | Onhand | SciSpace |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How you ask | Ask about the paper and Onhand finds the passages that answer it; you can also select text and ask | Highlight text, or clip an equation or table, to get an explanation, or ask about the whole article; then ask follow-ups |
| Showing sources | Highlights the passages on the page, with notes in the margin | A citation with each answer, with the location of the source |
| Finding papers | Follows the citations in the paper you're reading and opens the cited papers | Literature search across 280 million+ papers, and related papers for a highlighted passage |
| What it reads | Any webpage or PDF in Chrome: papers, course pages, docs and articles | Papers, articles and PDFs on most sites in Chrome, from publishers and arXiv to blogs and news |
| Saving your work | Sessions saved in your browser with their highlights and notes | Save articles and PDFs to a SciSpace collection |
| Price and code | Free tier with a daily limit, or your ChatGPT plan or own API key; open source | Extension free to install; paid plans from $12 a month billed yearly, or $20 monthly |

SciSpace details are from its [Chrome Web Store listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/scispace-do-hours-of-rese/cipccbpjpemcnijhjcdjmkjhmhniiick) and its [extension](https://scispace.com/copilot-chrome-plugin), [literature review](https://scispace.com/literature-review) and [pricing](https://scispace.com/pricing) pages, checked October 21, 2026. ## Why read papers with Onhand

-   **Questions about the whole paper:** "What's the main contribution?" or "Where do they define the loss?" Onhand finds the passages and highlights them, so you don't have to know where to look first.
-   **Citations followed:** ask what a reference says, and Onhand opens the cited paper and answers from it.
-   **Papers compared:** with two papers open, ask how they relate, and each claim is highlighted in the paper it came from.
-   **Learn it, not just skim it:** Learning mode asks guiding questions and quizzes you on the paper.

## When SciSpace fits better

For literature reviews, SciSpace searches its database of over 280 million papers and finds papers related to a passage. Onhand doesn't search a paper database; it reads the papers you open and the ones they cite.

## Using both

Find papers with SciSpace's search, then read each one closely with Onhand.

## Try it

Add Onhand to Chrome, open the page you're reading, and click the Onhand icon or press `Ctrl+Shift+Space` (`⌘+Shift+Space` on a Mac). Onhand Free needs no account or API key and has a daily limit; you can also sign in with your ChatGPT plan or use your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or OpenRouter key.
