# Onhand is installed.

Three steps to your first answer. Onhand reads the page you're on and answers by highlighting the passages it used, with notes beside them.

1.  ## Pin Onhand
    
    Chrome hides new extensions. Click the puzzle-piece **Extensions** button at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to **Onhand**.
    
2.  ## Open it on this page
    
    Click the Onhand icon, or press Ctrl Shift Space. The panel opens beside this page.
    
3.  ## Pick how it runs
    
    **Try Onhand free** needs no account or key, with a daily limit. You can also sign in with your ChatGPT plan or use your own API key.
    

Ask Onhand about this passage:

-   What's the main idea here?
-   How does spacing compare with cramming?
-   Select a sentence, then ask: Explain this simply.

## Why spacing out study works

In the 1880s, Hermann Ebbinghaus memorized lists of nonsense syllables and tested how quickly he forgot them. Most of the forgetting came soon after learning: within a day, much of a new list was gone, and the losses slowed after that. This pattern is now called the forgetting curve.

Ebbinghaus also found that reviewing a list made it quicker to relearn, and that spreading the same amount of review across several days kept more of it than cramming it into one sitting. Psychologists call this the spacing effect.

Later research added a second idea: recalling something from memory strengthens it more than rereading it does. Quizzing yourself, known as the testing effect, works best when the questions come back at spaced intervals. Together, spaced and retrieval-based review are among the most reliable study strategies researchers have found.

Turn on **Learning** at the bottom of the panel, then ask *Quiz me on this*: Onhand tutors with questions instead of handing you the answer.

## Where Onhand works

-   **Articles, docs and course pages:** open the page, then ask.
-   **PDFs:** Onhand opens them in its own viewer so it can highlight them.
-   **Not on the browser's own pages:** browsers don't let extensions read their add-on stores or pages like the new tab page.
