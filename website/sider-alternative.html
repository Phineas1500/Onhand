A Sider alternative built for reading and studying.

Onhand is a free, open-source AI Chrome extension for reading. Sider is an all-in-one AI sidebar for searching, reading and writing; Onhand focuses on the page in front of you. It highlights the passages behind each answer, leaves notes in the margin, and can tutor you with Learning mode.

Built for the page you're reading

  • Answers you can check: every point is highlighted where it comes from, so you read the evidence instead of trusting a summary.
  • Notes in the margin: a sentence or two beside each highlight, so you keep your place on the page.
  • Citations followed: ask what a reference like [14] says, and Onhand opens the cited paper and answers from it.
  • A tutor when you want one: Learning mode asks before it tells and quizzes you on the page.

When Sider fits better

Sider does much more outside reading: writing help in text boxes across sites, translating whole pages and PDFs, bilingual YouTube subtitles, AI answers alongside search engines, and comparing several models' answers side by side. If those are what you need most, Sider covers them; Onhand is focused on reading and learning.

Try it

Add Onhand to Chrome, open the page you're reading, and click the Onhand icon or press Ctrl+Shift+Space (⌘+Shift+Space on a Mac). Onhand Free needs no account or API key and has a daily limit; you can also sign in with your ChatGPT plan or use your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or OpenRouter key.
