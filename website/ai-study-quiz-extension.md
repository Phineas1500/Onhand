# An AI study tool that quizzes you on what you're reading.

Onhand is a free AI Chrome extension for studying. Open your reading, whether a textbook chapter, lecture notes, an article or a PDF, and ask Onhand to quiz you. It asks about that page, gives hints from the page when you're stuck, and when it grades your answer, highlights the passage that settles it.

[Add to Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ogjmncmkpgdkkcibdiacmagaehjohljb)

Free to start. No account needed. Open source. Also on [Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/onhand-ai/).

## "Quiz me on this"

Ask Onhand to quiz you and it writes questions from the page you have open, saying which section or figure each one covers. The page stays unmarked while you think, so a highlight can't give the answer away. Answer in the side panel and Onhand grades you, highlighting the passage that settles each answer. If you're stuck, its hints point you back to the evidence instead of giving the answer away. Say "quiz me as we go" and it keeps going: after each answer it teaches the next part, then asks the next question.

## Learning mode: a tutor in the margin

Turn on **Learning** at the bottom of the side panel and Onhand teaches instead of just answering:

-   It asks a guiding question about the passage before it explains.
-   It checks what you remember after an explanation.
-   On homework-style problems, it helps you work through the steps instead of handing you the final answer. Turn Learning off when you want the answer itself.
-   A **This session** panel lists the concepts you've covered and the questions still open, with links back to where each came from.

## Study materials from your reading

Ask for flashcards, a summary or a formula sheet from the page, and Onhand makes it from that material. With your lecture slides and textbook open in different tabs, it can draw on both.

## Where it works

Course pages, textbooks online, articles, docs and PDFs in Chrome, including pages you're signed in to, because Onhand reads the page in your browser. Try it on the [sample passage on our welcome page](https://useonhand.com/welcome.html).

## Try it

Add Onhand to Chrome, open the page you're reading, and click the Onhand icon or press `Ctrl+Shift+Space` (`⌘+Shift+Space` on a Mac). Onhand Free needs no account or API key and has a daily limit; you can also sign in with your ChatGPT plan or use your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or OpenRouter key.
