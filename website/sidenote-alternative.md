# A Sidenote alternative that marks up the page as it answers.

Onhand and Sidenote are both AI reading assistants that show where an answer comes from. In Sidenote, you click a citation to jump to the sentence it came from. Onhand highlights the passages as it answers, leaves a note in the margin beside each one, and can tutor you with Learning mode. It's free to start, needs no account, and is open source.

[Add to Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ogjmncmkpgdkkcibdiacmagaehjohljb)

Free to start. No account needed. Open source. Also on [Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/onhand-ai/).

## How Onhand and Sidenote differ

|  | Onhand | Sidenote |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Showing the source | Highlights the passages it used on the page as it answers, with a note in the margin beside each | Citation chips in the side panel; click one and the page scrolls to the sentence and highlights it |
| Where it runs | A side panel in Chrome and Edge, or a sidebar in Firefox, with its own PDF viewer | A side panel in Chrome, Edge and Firefox, plus a web dashboard and a Slack app; desktop apps are announced |
| What it reads | Webpages and PDFs, online or on your computer, and clearly related open tabs | Webpages, PDFs, YouTube, Gmail and Outlook, Google Docs and Drive, Notion, Confluence, SharePoint and OneDrive, and uploaded files such as Word and PowerPoint |
| Learning | Learning mode: guiding questions, hints and quizzes tied to the page; flashcards and summaries when you ask | Summaries, explanations at a chosen reading level and a glossary; flashcards and quizzes in its web dashboard on paid plans |
| AI models | Onhand Free, your ChatGPT plan, or your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or OpenRouter key | Anthropic's Claude models, chosen by Sidenote; the model depends on the plan |
| Account and data | No account for Onhand Free; saved sessions stay on your device | Sign-in with email, Google or GitHub; documents are processed and stored on Sidenote's servers unless you choose to discard them |
| Price and code | Free tier with a daily limit, or your ChatGPT plan or own API key; open source under Apache 2.0 | A free plan with monthly limits, and paid plans from £10 a month |

## Built for the page you're reading

-   **Evidence without clicking:** the passages behind an answer light up on the page while Onhand answers, so you can check each point where it's written.
-   **Notes in the margin:** a sentence or two beside each highlight, so you keep your place on the page.
-   **Citations followed:** ask what a reference like \[14\] says, and Onhand opens the cited paper and answers from it.
-   **A tutor when you want one:** Learning mode asks before it tells and quizzes you on the page.

## When Sidenote fits better

Sidenote reaches more places. It reads Google Docs and Drive, Notion, Confluence, SharePoint and OneDrive, and it works with Gmail and Outlook, YouTube videos with timestamped citations, Word and PowerPoint uploads, and scanned PDFs with OCR on paid plans. It also keeps a searchable library you can ask across, has a web dashboard and a Slack app, and offers team plans; desktop apps are announced. If you work mostly in those tools, Sidenote covers them; Onhand is focused on the webpages and PDFs you read in your browser.

## Try it

Add Onhand to Chrome, open the page you're reading, and click the Onhand icon or press `Ctrl+Shift+Space` (`⌘+Shift+Space` on a Mac). Onhand Free needs no account or API key and has a daily limit; you can also sign in with your ChatGPT plan or use your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or OpenRouter key.
